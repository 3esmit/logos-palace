# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Logos Palace contributors, maintainers, and community participants commit to a
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project environment.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code on their technical merits.
- Assume good intent while asking for concrete evidence.
- Give actionable, respectful feedback.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and different levels of experience.
- Accept correction and repair harm when a discussion goes wrong.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publication of another
person's private information are not accepted.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, review
threads, project events, and public spaces where someone represents the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[`3esmit@gmail.com`](mailto:3esmit@gmail.com). Do not use a public issue when
doing so could expose a target or sensitive evidence.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors
whose conduct is incompatible with this policy. Reports will be reviewed as
privately and promptly as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is itself
a violation.
